# Curated comparisons, not claimed influences

Keep two lists distinct: **inspected references** (R IDs, actual file/link,
Observed findings, and separately evidenced approval scope) and **suggested
comparisons** (C IDs, Proposed, based on a named input/observation or stated
intent). A familiar artist/studio/IP comparison does not mean that a specific
image was inspected. Never fabricate a URL, image, authorship, source, influence,
or approval. Say "comparable in [dimension]", not "influenced by", unless the
creator/source explicitly supplied that history.

For art-brief, curate **3–5 artist/studio/IP comparisons where evidence supports**
and they help communicate the direction. Fewer or none is correct when the fit
is weak, the bar is already tightly fixed, or the task is trivial. Do not force
a quota, pad with famous names, or substitute a comp list for missing approved
references. This is a focused adaptation of image-decomp's comparison discipline,
not its larger reference/pitch quota or an instruction to run its full report.

Each suggested comparison uses:

- **C ID / name — Proposed comparison**, source/basis, inspection status.
- **Borrow — Proposed:** exact dimension and visible/stated feature (silhouette,
  edge treatment, palette, texture, lighting, staging, tone, etc.).
- **Avoid — Proposed:** specific traits not being borrowed, and why they would
  mislead this brief. If no defensible avoid exists, say Unresolved or omit the
  candidate rather than invent a ban.
- **Why:** how the named dimension serves this asset's stated intent/read.

Borrow/avoid recommendations stay Proposed even if the comparison's source was
Observed or the user supplied the IP name. User-supplied borrow/avoid decisions
retain their actual status/source. A proposed avoid is not an approved negative
reference or a command; a described avoid is not an inspected negative image.
Keep supplied reference IDs and approval boundaries unchanged in condensed text
and prompts. Never use a comparison to redesign Brief-driven or Volume work.

Optionally offer **1–2 dimension-specific X-meets-Y pitches** when they sharpen
communication: **Proposed — X's [dimension] meets Y's [different dimension]**, with
an evidence/basis note for each half. No bare "X + Y" name-drops. Do not imply
influence, merge incompatible requirements, or resolve uncertainty by blending.
For fixed-direction work, pitches explain the stated bar rather than replace it.
Cut the pitch if either half lacks support or if it adds noise.

For outsource-intake, stay lighter: **1–2 Proposed comparisons only if useful**,
with compact borrow/avoid/why and source/inspection status. Do not require new
reference research. Omit enrichment for a routine internal crate or similarly
simple ask unless the requester specifically needs visual comparison. Offer
art-brief for deeper treatment rather than importing its full reference package.

In debug, the main skill's no-lookup rule applies: use supplied inputs or mark
reference slots Unresolved; never pretend an unseen reference was inspected.
